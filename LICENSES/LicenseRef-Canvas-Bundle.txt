Third-party bundle

The files in canvas/src/boring_canvas/assets/ are a build of Excalidraw and
its dependencies, with their fonts, made by canvas/frontend/build.mjs. They
are not covered by the licence of boring-services. Each component keeps its
own licence: the inventory with versions and declared licences is
assets/DEPENDENCIES.json, the licence texts the packages ship are in
assets/licenses/, and esbuild's extracted legal comments are in the
*.LEGAL.txt files next to the chunks; each font directory has its
LICENSE.txt. canvas/frontend/notices/ keeps the notices upstream does not
ship. BUILD.json records the build.
